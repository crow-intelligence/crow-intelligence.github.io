The Nagel Index measures the gap between the public and private personality of historical figures — the Big Five traits a leader shows in their formal speeches versus the ones they reveal in their private letters.

The methodology — corpora, scoring, and uncertainty — is described in the companion essay, The Nagel Index: public mask, private letter.
