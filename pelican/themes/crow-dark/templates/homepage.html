Crow Intelligence is a boutique research and advisory firm working where language, cognition, and AI meet.

Some questions you are paid to answer; some you study because they will not let you go. We treat both with the same method — computational analysis joined to cognitive science — and the same standard of proof.

Independent, deliberately small, and committed to working in the open: our essays and our tools are public, and our client work is the better for it.
