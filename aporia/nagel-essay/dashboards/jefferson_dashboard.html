The Nagel Index measures the gap between the public and private personality of historical figures — the Big Five traits a leader shows in their formal speeches against the ones they reveal in their private letters.

This dashboard covers Thomas Jefferson. The method — the corpora, the scoring, and how uncertainty is handled — is set out in the companion essay, The Nagel Index: does power change a person?
